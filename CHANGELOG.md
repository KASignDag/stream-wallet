# Changelog

## 0.3.11

- Added a scannable receiving-address QR code.
- Moved recipient QR scanning inside the Send flow.
- Added device-authenticated recovery-phrase display.
- Added local outgoing transaction history and an explicit history limitation.
- Distinguished total, spendable and maturing balances.
- Fixed iOS safe areas, form zoom and scanner lifecycle locking.
- Migrated the native shells to Capacitor 8.

This remains a controlled mainnet pilot. See `docs/TEST_STATUS.md` and
`docs/RELEASE_GATES.md` for verified and unfinished work.
